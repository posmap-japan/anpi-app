1. The Service

The App helps you watch over the people you care about, based on Apple Health step data, use of the App and replies to safety checks. It is used with the mutual consent of the watcher and the person being watched. Main features:

  • Activity confirmation from step increases, use of the App, and replies to "Are you OK?" messages
  • Notifications to the watcher and to the person themselves when no activity is confirmed for a set time
  • Separate notifications when no data is arriving, distinguished from "no activity"
  • Watch time settings (1 to 24 hours)
  • Sending and receiving "Are you OK?" messages
  • Safety alerts by push notification
  • A Home Screen widget showing the time since each person's last activity

2. Information Collected

The App collects only the minimum information needed to provide the service: display name and profile photo; step data from Apple Health with your permission (used only to confirm when steps increased; step details are not sent to the server); activity times (when steps increased, when you opened the App, when and how you replied to an "Are you OK?" message, and when data was received); operating status; pairing settings; and a push notification token. See the Privacy Policy for details.

We never collect location, contacts, call history, message content, browsing history, use of other apps, or sleep data. Step details are never shared with the people you are paired with.

3. Use of Information

Information is used only to provide the watch feature, authenticate users, manage accounts, and maintain and improve the service.

We never use information for advertising or sell it to third parties.

4. Storage and Security

  1. Data is stored on Firebase (Google Cloud).
  2. Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (SSL/TLS).
  3. Activity records kept to prevent duplicates are deleted automatically after 30 days.
  4. When you delete your account, all related data is deleted promptly.
  5. The App never collects or stores payment details. Payments are processed through the App Store and RevenueCat.

5. How Activity Is Confirmed

For the person being watched, the App treats any of the following as activity:

  • An increase in Apple Health steps (only if the user has explicitly allowed access to Health)
  • Opening and using the App
  • Replying to an "Are you OK?" message in the App. Both "I'm OK" and "I'm not OK" count as activity, because they confirm the person used their phone. The reply is sent to the watcher.
  • Location (GPS) is not used. Use of other apps and sleep data are not used to confirm activity.
  • When steps are sent depends on the device's state (power, connection, Low Power Mode, Background App Refresh, etc.) and may be delayed.
  • If no data arrives because the device is off or offline, a "no data arriving" notification is sent. Notifications may be sent even if the person is actually active.
  • The App does not medically distinguish between normal rest and illness.
