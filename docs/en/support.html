About Anpi

Anpi helps you check on family and loved ones who live apart, without using location (GPS). If no activity is confirmed for a time you set, Anpi notifies you, and you can send an "Are you OK?" message to check in.

  • See your contact's last activity and whether data is arriving
  • Set a watch time (1 to 24 hours) and get automatic alerts when no activity is confirmed
  • Send "Are you OK?" and receive "I'm OK" / "I'm not OK" replies
  • Pair easily with an invite code
  • Home Screen widget (time since last activity; medium and large sizes show several people)
  • Notifications, Steps and About screens in Settings to check and change permissions

How watching works

Anpi does not use location (GPS). Any of the following counts as activity and resets the watch timer:

  • Steps: when Apple Health steps increase (if Steps is allowed in Health). Steps are sent automatically when iPhone allows, even if Anpi isn't open.
  • Using Anpi: when the person being watched opens Anpi.
  • Replying to "Are you OK?": replying "I'm OK" or "I'm not OK" in Anpi. Either reply resets the timer, and the reply is sent to the watcher. If the reply is "I'm not OK", please get in touch right away.
  • Alerts: if no activity is confirmed for the set time (1 to 24 hours), both the watcher and the person being watched are notified.
  • When no data arrives: if the phone is off or offline, Anpi sends a separate "no data arriving" notification.
  • Not detected: use of other apps (messaging apps, browsers, etc.) or simply unlocking the phone does not count. Sleep data is not used.
  • Not a medical judgment: Anpi cannot tell rest apart from illness.

FAQ

Q. What resets the timer?

An increase in steps, opening Anpi, or replying to "Are you OK?" (either "I'm OK" or "I'm not OK"). Steps are sent automatically even if Anpi isn't open, and the timer uses the time you actually walked.

Q. Does using messaging apps or a browser reset the timer?

No. Anpi does not detect the use of other apps or unlocking. The timer resets when steps increase, when Anpi is opened, or when "Are you OK?" is answered.

Q. I got a "no data arriving" notification.

No data has arrived from your contact's phone for a while. The phone may be off or offline, or Anpi's settings may be off. It doesn't mean they are inactive, so please call or message them to check.

Q. I walked, but it isn't reflected.
  • In Health → Sharing → Apps → Anpi, make sure Steps is on
  • In Settings → General → Background App Refresh, make sure Anpi is on
  • Make sure Low Power Mode is off (sending may be delayed while it's on)

iPhone decides when steps are sent, so it may take some time. Opening Anpi sends them immediately. You can check whether Steps is on in Anpi's Settings → Steps.

Q. Can I add a widget to my Home Screen?

Yes. Touch and hold the Home Screen → "+" → Anpi. It shows each person's photo, name and time since their last activity; medium and large sizes show several people. It updates every few minutes to every few tens of minutes, and immediately when you open Anpi.

Q. Can Anpi tell whether someone is resting or unwell?

No. Anpi is not a medical device. It only checks whether the set time has passed since the last confirmed activity. In an emergency, always contact emergency services.

Q. Do I need a paid plan?

You can watch over 1 person with the Free plan. To watch over 2 or more people, you need Plus, Family or Business. No plan is needed to be watched.

Q. The invite code doesn't work.

Invite codes are valid for 7 days. If a code has expired, the person who invited you can open the invite screen again to get a new one. After repeated wrong entries, input may be blocked for a while.

Q. How do I cancel my subscription?

Go to iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions, choose Anpi and cancel.

Q. What if I change phones?

Sign in with the same Apple ID and tap "Restore Purchases" in the App to restore your subscription.

Q. Notifications don't arrive.

On the receiving iPhone, check Settings → Notifications → Anpi and make sure "Allow Notifications" and "Banners" are on. If they still don't arrive, update Anpi to the latest version and sign in again.

Q. How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings → Delete Account in the App. All data, including activity records and safety checks, is deleted and cannot be restored.
