Anpi

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Anpi ("the App") collects, how it is used and how it is protected. The Japanese version of this policy is the governing version; this English version is provided for convenience.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account information

  • Authentication information from Sign in with Apple or Google (such as your display name; we do not ask you to enter an email address)
  • Profile information you choose to set

1.2 Health information (for watching)

  • Step data read from Apple Health, used to confirm when your steps increased
  • Step details are not sent to our servers; only the time your steps increased (the recorded time range) is sent
  • Collected only if you explicitly allow access in Health. Sleep data is not read
  • Location (GPS) is never collected

1.3 Information related to watching

  • Your connections with the people you watch or who watch you (pairings)
  • Time and type of activity (when your steps increased, when you opened the App, and when and how you replied to an "Are you OK?" message)
  • When data was received (to tell "no data arriving" apart from "no activity confirmed")
  • Records of safety-check messages sent and received
  • Watch time settings

1.4 Device information

  • A device token for push notifications (FCM token)
  • Device type, OS version, app version and language setting
  • Step reading and sending status, Background App Refresh and Low Power Mode status (to understand why data might not arrive)

1.5 Purchase information

  • Subscription status (plan and expiry)
  • Payments are processed by Apple (App Store) and RevenueCat. The App does not collect or store payment details such as credit card numbers.

1.6 Information we do not collect

  • We never collect location (GPS), contacts, call history, message content, browsing history, use of other apps, or sleep data

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide the watch service based on steps, use of the App and replies to safety checks
  • To notify watchers and the person themselves when no activity is confirmed for a set time, or when no data is arriving
  • To authenticate users and manage accounts
  • To manage subscriptions
  • To improve the App and provide support
  • To respond to violations of the Terms of Service

3. Sharing of Information

We do not provide your personal information to third parties except as follows.

  • People you are paired with: Only with people you have mutually agreed to pair with, the information needed for watching is shared (display name, profile photo, time and type of your last activity, whether data is arriving, watch time, and your replies to safety checks). Detailed step data itself is never shared. When a pairing is removed, the other person can no longer see this information.
  • Service providers: Firebase (Google), RevenueCat and other services needed to operate the App.
  • Legal requirements: When required by law or legal process.

4. Storage and Security

  • Data is stored securely in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore).
  • Communication is encrypted with SSL/TLS.
  • Access is restricted so that only you and the people you are paired with can see your information.
  • Activity records kept to prevent duplicates are deleted automatically after 30 days.
  • When you delete your account, related data such as activity records, safety checks and notifications is deleted promptly.

5. Your Rights

  • Access: You may request disclosure of your data.
  • Correction: You may request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Deletion: You can delete all of your data by deleting your account in the App's settings.
  • Notifications: You can turn off push notifications in your device settings.

Depending on where you live (for example, the EU/EEA, the UK or California), you may have additional rights under local law, such as data portability or objecting to processing. Contact us to exercise them.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. International Transfers

The App is operated from Japan, and data may be processed in Japan, the United States or other countries where our service providers operate.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as needed. If there are important changes, we will let you know in the App or on this page.
