Anpi is a safety check-in app that watches over parents and loved ones who live apart, without GPS tracking or cameras.
It only alerts family when someone's steps haven't increased for the time you set. No daily action is needed.

Important
Anpi is not an emergency service or a medical device. In an emergency, contact local emergency services right away.

We don't collect extra data
• Watching never uses location (GPS), the camera or the microphone (the camera is only for taking a profile photo)
• No contacts, messages, browsing history, use of other apps, or sleep data
• Only the time steps increased is used. Step details are never sent or shared
• Watching starts only when both people agree
• All communication is encrypted, and we never use your information for ads or sell it

How watching works
• Any of these counts as activity and resets the watch timer:
  – Steps increase (from Apple Health and iPhone's motion sensor; sent automatically, even when Anpi is closed or your iPhone is locked)
  – Opening Anpi
  – Replying to "Are you OK?" (either "I'm OK" or "I'm not OK")
• If no activity is confirmed for the set time (12–36 hours; 24 by default), both the watcher and the person being watched are notified
• If there's still no response, Anpi repeats the alert every hour, up to 3 more times (with a 24-hour setting: at 24, 25, 26 and 27 hours)
• If no data is arriving from the phone, Anpi sends a separate "no data arriving" notice instead of a "no activity" alert
• Because of how iPhone works, updates can be delayed by tens of minutes to an hour while the app is closed, so walking doesn't reset the timer instantly
• Unlocking the phone or using other apps does not reset the timer
• Anpi does not medically tell rest apart from illness

Features
• Send "Are you OK?" and get a one-tap reply
• Get notified right away when the reply is "I'm not OK"
• Home Screen widget: photo, name and time since last activity with a gauge (medium and large sizes show several people)
• Two kinds of invites — "I want to watch over you" and "Please watch over me" — with easy invite-code pairing
• "This iPhone's settings" in Settings shows Notifications, Steps, Motion & Fitness and Background App Refresh at a glance. Watching starts once everything is on

Great for
• People worried about parents or grandparents living alone
• Families who live far apart and find daily check-in calls a burden
• Anyone who wants to watch over someone without tracking location or using cameras
• Landlords and property managers checking on tenants

Plans (the number is how many people you can watch over)
• Free — 1 person (free)
• Plus — up to 2 people. Great for most households
• Family — up to 5 people. Great for parents and relatives
• Business — up to 50 people. Great for landlords and property managers
