Important
Anpi is not an emergency service or a medical device. In a real emergency, call local emergency services right away.

Anpi helps you watch over people you care about—using steps and sleep data from Apple Health, not GPS.

How monitoring works
• Anpi does not use location tracking
• The person being watched allows Apple Health access to Steps and Sleep
• Anpi updates a “last activity time” from the newer of:
  – the latest Steps sample, or
  – the end time of the latest Sleep Analysis sample
• If there is no new activity for the time you set (1–24 hours), the watcher gets a notification
• Sleep records help reduce false alerts overnight, when someone is resting and not walking
• Anpi does not medically distinguish normal sleep from illness or collapse—it only checks whether activity records have stopped for the set time
• Unlocking the phone alone does not reset the timer; updates happen when Health data is available and synced

Invite in two ways
• “I want to watch over you” — invite someone you want to monitor
• “I want you to watch over me” — invite someone to look after you

Other features
• Send a simple “Are you OK?” check-in
• Easy pairing with an invitation code
• Monitor multiple people on Family / Business plans

Privacy-minded by design
• No GPS / no live location sharing
• Steps and sleep details are not shared with the watcher—only activity timing needed for monitoring
• Monitoring starts only after both sides agree
