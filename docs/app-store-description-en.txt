Important
Anpi is not an emergency service or a medical device. In an emergency, contact local emergency services right away.

Anpi helps you gently watch over family and loved ones who live apart, without using GPS. If no activity is confirmed for the time you set, Anpi lets you know.

How watching works
• No location tracking
• Any of these counts as activity and resets the watch timer:
  – Apple Health steps increase (sent automatically, even when Anpi isn't open)
  – Opening Anpi
  – Replying to "Are you OK?" (either "I'm OK" or "I'm not OK")
• If no activity is confirmed for the set time (1–24 hours), both the watcher and the person being watched are notified
• If no data is arriving from the phone, Anpi sends a separate "no data arriving" notice instead of a "no activity" alert
• Unlocking the phone or using other apps does not reset the timer
• Anpi does not medically tell rest apart from illness

Features
• Send "Are you OK?" and get a one-tap reply
• Get notified right away when the reply is "I'm not OK"
• Home Screen widget: photo, name and time since last activity with a gauge (medium and large sizes show several people)
• Two kinds of invites — "I want to watch over you" and "Please watch over me" — with easy invite-code pairing
• Check notification and Steps status right from Settings

Privacy
• We never collect location, contacts, messages, browsing history, use of other apps, or sleep data
• Step details are never sent or shared — only the time your steps increased is used
• Watching starts only when both people agree
• All communication is encrypted, and we never use your information for ads or sell it

Plans (the number is how many people you can watch over)
• Free — 1 person (free)
• Plus — up to 2 people
• Family — up to 5 people
• Business — up to 50 people
Prices are shown in the App Store in your local currency. No plan is needed to be watched.
